The idea

Plain explanation of the idea, in short sentences. Define each new term at its first use.

An everyday comparison. A coat check gives you a ticket for your coat. The ticket is the key, and the coat is the value. The comparison stops here: a coat check has one coat per ticket, but a store can replace the value under a key.

What can go wrong

  • Two keys, one slot. Two keys can give the same slot. The store must keep both values.

What do you need to get a value back from a key-value store?

The key. The store keeps each value under its key.

 Where does the coat-check comparison stop being true?

A store can replace the value under a key. A coat check gives one coat per ticket.
